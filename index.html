The cash-timing gap

Why this business is working-capital hungry — and a financing question, not just a P&L one.

Cash goes out early (biomass, reactor build, production, MRV); credits are only issued, delivered and paid over a verification lag. The trough is the financing need — what equity and debt have to bridge.

Three levers I'd pressure-test

Framed as questions, not prescriptions — I'd want the actuals before claiming any of these.

  • Defend the cost-base moat.India is among the lowest-cost durable-removal supply globally while credits price off a global curve. How much of that spread is structural vs. eroding as Indian supply scales — and how do we protect it in offtake pricing?
  • Shorten the cash-timing gap.Can pre-payments / milestone-linked offtake tranches or a verification-linked debt facility shrink the working-capital trough, lowering the cost of capital per tonne?
  • Mix toward margin, not just volume.Enhanced rock weathering prices ~2x biochar. What does a contribution-per-tonne view (not tonnes alone) say about where the next reactor / project dollar should go?
